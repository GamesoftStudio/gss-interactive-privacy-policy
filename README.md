# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 15/10/2026 
**Developer/Publisher:** GSS Interactive

GSS Interactive ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you play our games or use our related services.

This policy applies to games developed or published by GSS Interactive unless a game provides its own separate privacy policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on the game, platform, and services integrated into it.

### Information Collected Automatically

Our games or the third-party services integrated into them may automatically collect certain information, including:

- Device type, operating system, and device specifications.
- IP address and approximate location derived from it.
- Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers.
- Game performance data, crash reports, and diagnostic information.
- Gameplay activity and interaction data, where analytics services are used.
- Advertising impressions, clicks, and related interaction data.

We do not intentionally collect personal information directly from players unless a game feature or service specifically requires it.

## 2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Some of our games may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks or mediation platforms, such as Unity LevelPlay, ironSource, Google AdMob, or other providers.

These services may collect and process information such as advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, diagnostic data, and advertising interactions to deliver advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and support other advertising-related functions.

The providers and data collected may vary between games.

Third-party services process information under their own privacy policies and applicable terms. We encourage players to review the privacy policies of the services used in the relevant game.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Only the services actually integrated into a particular game should be considered applicable.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information collected through our games and associated services may be used to:

- Operate and maintain our games.
- Improve game performance, stability, and user experience.
- Identify and fix technical issues.
- Display and measure advertisements.
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and activities that could compromise our services.
- Understand game usage and improve future updates.
- Meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information directly to third parties. However, third-party advertising providers may process information for their own purposes as described in their respective privacy policies.

## 4. Data Sharing

Information may be processed or shared with the following categories of recipients, where applicable:

- **Advertising providers:** To serve advertisements, measure performance, and prevent advertising fraud.
- **Analytics and diagnostic providers:** To understand game performance and troubleshoot technical problems.
- **Platform providers:** Such as Steam, Google Play, or other distribution platforms, which may process information under their own policies.
- **Legal authorities:** When disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary to protect legal rights and safety.

We do not share information with third parties beyond what is necessary for the relevant purposes, as permitted by applicable law and the services used.

## 5. Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements and the retention practices of third-party providers.

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Information handled independently by third-party providers is also subject to their own retention and security practices.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our games are not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

Where a game is intended for children or may be accessed by children, we will take appropriate steps to comply with applicable requirements concerning children's privacy, advertising, and parental consent.

Parents or legal guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately may contact us using the details below.

## 7. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of certain personal information.

You may also be able to manage advertising personalization through your device settings or the privacy controls provided by the relevant advertising service.

For games distributed through third-party platforms, some account and purchase information may need to be managed directly through the relevant platform.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the information provided below. We may need to verify your request before taking action.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Our games may use third-party services that process information in countries other than your country of residence. Where this occurs, information may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions.

We expect applicable service providers to handle information in accordance with their own policies and relevant legal requirements.

## 9. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our games or websites may contain links to third-party websites or services. We do not control their privacy practices or content.

Any information you provide directly to those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.
